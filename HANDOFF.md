# Independent operation and community handoff

There is no founder-operated payment service, hosted wallet, mining pool,
guaranteed infrastructure, development contract or promised ongoing support.
No automatic founder payment exists. The original publisher may participate
as an ordinary miner under the same protocol as everyone else.

The MIT license lets anyone copy, modify and redistribute the software while
retaining the notices. No founder approval is required to run a node, mine,
review the code or maintain a fork. The chain rules are enforced by participants,
not by the website or a founder-controlled activation file.

## Bootstrap coordination

The initial verified bootstrap list is empty. Independent operators can publish
their own opt-in public P2P endpoints in the repository's public Discussions.
That is public coordination among participants, not an application to the founder.
An endpoint must be intentionally public, run this exact network identity and
use TCP 31444. Publish only an IP/hostname and P2P port that you operate; no wallet
RPC address, cookies, wallet files, keys or recovery material.

Readers must independently verify endpoints, network genesis and observed chain
work. Publicly advertised peers can be malicious or unavailable. Do not regard
the founder's silence, an endpoint post, or a node's height as network approval.

Before describing the coin as a functioning public network, volunteers need to
demonstrate cross-host reachability, synchronization, block propagation, wallet
receipts and restart persistence. Software-build tests alone do not prove these.

## Maintenance and continuity

Volunteers may maintain their own repositories, releases, mirrors and peer lists.
Existing repository/domain access is not transferred merely by publishing code.
No maintainer takeover, volunteer node, support commitment or funding source is
claimed until an actual volunteer establishes one. The website/domain can cease
to be maintained; keep independently verified source and release hashes.

## No commercial or legal clearance

This release neither opens a sale nor promises price appreciation, liquidity or
exchange support. Publishing software does not remove any obligations arising
from prior conduct, representations, custody or sales. Anyone planning commercial
activity must evaluate their actual jurisdiction and business model separately.
